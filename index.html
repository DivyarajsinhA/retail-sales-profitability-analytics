ANALYTICAL FINDINGS

Key Business Insights & Findings

Analytical observations identified from the retail dataset, paired with actionable business recommendations.

INSIGHT 01 – DISCOUNT-DRIVEN PROFIT MARGIN EROSION

23.1 pp Margin Drop on 20%+ Discounted Orders

Full-price orders achieved a 36.0% profit margin, while orders receiving standard discounts of 5%–15% achieved 27.8%. Orders with discounts of 20% or higher (107 orders) had a significantly lower profit margin of 12.9%—a 23.1 percentage-point difference.

Business Recommendation: Review high-discount promotional strategies, especially in Furniture, to reduce unnecessary profit margin erosion.
INSIGHT 02 – REGIONAL SALES PERFORMANCE

West Leads Revenue; Central Leads Margin

The West region generated the highest reported revenue of ₹1.01 crore across 320 orders, followed by the South region with ₹87.9 lakh across 305 orders. The Central region achieved the highest reported regional profit margin of 30.8%.

Business Recommendation: Study the sales strategies of high-performing regions and identify opportunities to improve profitability across other regions.
INSIGHT 03 – PRODUCT CATEGORY PROFITABILITY

High-Volume Electronics vs. High-Margin Merch

Electronics generated the highest reported category revenue of ₹1.40 crore, with a profit margin of 24.8%. Corporate Merch achieved a profit margin of 42.5%, and Office Supplies achieved 37.6%.

Business Recommendation: Monitor product category performance and balance revenue growth with profitability.
INSIGHT 04 – YEAR-OVER-YEAR REVENUE GROWTH

+33.8% Annual Revenue Expansion (2024–2025)

Revenue increased by 33.8% between 2024 (₹93.4 lakh) and 2025 (₹1.25 crore). 2026 YTD revenue (January–October) stands at ₹1.14 crore across 420 orders.

Business Recommendation: Continue tracking revenue trends and investigate seasonal performance and growth opportunities.
